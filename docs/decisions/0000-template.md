# Title

State the decision in a short phrase, prefixed with the record number, for example "0001 Use property bags for content tables".

## Status

Proposed, Accepted, or Superseded by a later record.

## Date

The date the status last changed, as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Context

The problem, the constraints and the options considered, in a few sentences. Link the issue.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two sentences, starting with "We will".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work this creates.
