## Changes that break code built before this preview

- The prune of failed outbox rows and the purge of a handler's dead letters seek the rows they remove, on two new indexes of `cms_outbox` filtered to `status = 'failed'`: `ix_cms_outbox_failed_available_at` on `(available_at, attempts)` and `ix_cms_outbox_failed_handler` on `handler` (#3411). They went into the core `Initial`, since a preview upgrades no database. A database made by `1.0.0-preview.1` lacks them and still starts, since the startup check refuses a missing column and not a missing index, but its prune and purge read every failed row as before; recreate it to have them.

## Additions

- The outbox drain gives the rows of a batch back with one write, for a stopping host, a failed renewal, hand-offs that never started and handlers that are missing or whose plugin is disabled, where it wrote once for each row (#3410). The writes of a stopping host share `JobRunnerOptions.ReleaseTimeout` (5 seconds) in all, with the delivered rows of each lane settled first and in half of it at most, so that the release always has its turn, also when the stop comes during the read of which plugins are disabled (#3521). The rows of a write that does not fit keep their lease and the attempt of their claim, and are delivered when the lease ends, one per claim by another instance and again for those already delivered, which is at-least-once. The sweep fails instead a row that was on its last attempt, one whose own write did not fit or, when the release itself could not be written, one no handler saw: it is in the dead-letter list and can be retried. When which plugins are disabled cannot be read, the batch goes back after one read, and its lane waits for the next signal. Rows that a claim of a running instance gave up without a write are claimed again by that instance with the rest of their batch, and the drain leaves out at most `BatchSize` deferred rows, the latest.
