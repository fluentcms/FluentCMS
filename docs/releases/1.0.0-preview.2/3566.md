## Fixes

- A read that falls back, a list with or without `q` or an entry read by id that has a row in the requested locale, answers when a field limit of the caller's grant in a fallback locale leaves out some of the fields the entry lacks: that locale fills only the fields the caller may read in it, and a field it may not read there falls back further along the chain or stays empty. It refused the whole read with 400 `FCMS2202` on `fields`, also when the caller's own `fields` selection was valid in the requested locale; such a selection now answers. A `fields` selection that names a field the caller may not read in the requested locale is still refused with `FCMS2202` (#3566).
