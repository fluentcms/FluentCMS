## Additions

- A claim of the outbox drain that fails or is stopped after its update, before its rows are read back, gives them back with the attempt it counted. They stayed leased with one attempt more, and the sweep failed those on their last attempt though no handler saw them (#3537).
- No outbox handler starts once its lane is told that the host is stopping, also when the stop comes during a renewal of the lease or just before the hand-off. Such a handler could start after the drain returned, while its row was given back (#3537).
