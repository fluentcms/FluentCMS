## Changes that break code built before this preview

- A content list with `q` that falls back, or that reads `locale=all`, asks the search index once, in the locale it searches, and reads the other locales of the entries found by their ids. Their fields now fall back to a locale whose text does not hold the words, and a `locale=all` list holds every locale of each entry that the caller may read in it, where both left out what did not match. The entries listed, their order, `total`, `nextCursor` and `searchTruncated` are unchanged, and the Delivery and Management APIs, GraphQL, MCP and `IContentService` answer alike. Before, the index was asked again in each other locale and each batch of ids, and each of those answers was cut at `MaxSearchResults` without `searchTruncated` saying so. With an index whose `MatchesByField` is `false`, which the search plugin's is not, a `locale=all` list with `q` for a caller who may read every searchable field in the locale searched but not in another locale answers, where the search in that other locale refused the whole list with `FCMS2211` (#3469).
