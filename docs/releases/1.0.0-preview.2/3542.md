## Additions

- A content hook that had not ended when its caller was cancelled, such as by a client that went away or a lost `Idempotency-Key`, is logged as a warning with the new event 9408, naming the hook, its kind and its type; only a hook that timed out was logged, with event 9406 (#3542).
- A write whose rollback fails while a hook it left holds the connection in a command, as a driver may while that hook's reader is open (SqlClient without MARS refuses any other command then), fails with what it failed with, its cancellation or `FCMS2410`, instead of the driver's refusal, and is logged with the new event 9409; its transaction commits nothing and ends when the scope closes its connection (#3542).
