## Additions

- A content hook that had not ended when its caller was cancelled, such as by a client that went away or a lost `Idempotency-Key`, is logged as a warning with the new event 9408, naming the hook, its kind and its type; only a hook that timed out was logged, with event 9406 (#3542).
- On SQL Server without MARS, a write whose hook was left inside a command, its reader open, fails with what it failed with, its cancellation or `FCMS2410`, instead of SqlClient's refusal of the rollback beside that reader, which is logged with the new event 9409. Its transaction and its locks then stay until the scope is disposed, which rolls them back; nothing of it commits (#3542).
