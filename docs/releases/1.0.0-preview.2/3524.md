## Additions

- Writes, publishes, unpublishes, schedules, scheduled jobs, imports, trash, restore and purge of one entry take its rows in one order, its `cms_document` rows first and ordinal by locale code, so two of them that cross wait for each other. On PostgreSQL and SQL Server a publish of one locale and a shared write through another, a scheduled job and a write that ends its schedule, or an import and a shared write could deadlock, and one of them ran again after about one second on PostgreSQL and five on SQL Server (#3524).
- A save, a discard and a version restore decide their own status from what is stored once their swap holds the row, so a publish or an unpublish of that locale, or another locale's publish of the shared row, that committed between the write's read and its swap no longer leaves a stale status, such as `Modified` for a draft equal to what is published, `Draft` for a locale published meanwhile, or `Modified` for one unpublished meanwhile (#3524).
- A publish holds every locale row of its entry until it commits, so a write of another locale of that entry waits for the publish to end (#3524).
