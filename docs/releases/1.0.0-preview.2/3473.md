## Additions

- The answer of `POST /api/management/v1/content/{type}/{id}/preview-token` has `expiresIn`, the token's lifetime in whole seconds as a JSON integer: `expiresAt` less the token's `iat`, the requested `minutes` or `FluentCms:PreviewTokens:DefaultLifetime`. A client whose clock is not the server's, or one on another origin that cannot read the `Date` header, ends the token that long after its own request (#3473).
