## Additions

- The counter `fluentcms.rate_limit.refused` counts the requests that the six rate limits refuse with a 429, by `fluentcms.policy`: `pre-auth`, `auth`, `health`, `delivery`, `management` or `caller`. The limit of client registrations per hour answers 429 too and is not counted. Before, a refusal showed only as a 4xx in `fluentcms.requests` (#3385).
