## Additions

- A stopping host's job runner returns within `JobRunnerOptions.ReleaseTimeout` (5 seconds) after the grace period, also when a cancelled job writes its release inside the cancellation, as on SQLite while the command waits for the lock. Such a release held the stop for up to the command timeout of each such job before the time of the releases even began. Each worker's job is told of the stop on a thread of its own, so a release written so, or a cancellation callback that blocks, no longer delays telling the others, the outbox drain included. A job whose release is not written by then keeps its lease, is taken over once the lease ends and is never marked done, and event 9514 is logged (#3538).
